Zoonie

Zoonie is A Cloud Platform which has been established by Om Shri Siddhi Vinayak Foundation Prayagraj Uttar Pradesh. It is a noble & humble project dedicated to humanity. It is a Trademark registered. Zoonie was launched two years back in 2024 under the supervision, guidance and financial support by Om Shri Siddhi Vinayak Foundation, Prayagraj. The Trust was registered on 14/09/2010 at Prayagraj. Since 2010 the trust is providing its services to the society. Especially to the poor and middle class. The above Trust has Tax Examption Certificates, CSR Certificate, LEI Certificate, ISO Certificate and many more. The motto behind the establishment of Zoonie is to bring down the high cost of treatment and make it relevant for a common man and to eliminate the middle man/brokers from the hospital industry.
